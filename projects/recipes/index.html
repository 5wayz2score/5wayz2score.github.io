Cinnamon Pancakes

The Story Behind the Recipe

I selected cinnamon pancakes because they make a delicious breakfast for weekends and special occasions, especially when paired with scrambled eggs, bacon, and orange juice.Their homemade flavor makes them a perfect way to start my day.

In my example, pancakes represent weekend mornings. When me and my mom sit at the dining room table, we're able to talk and laugh over a mouthwatering breakfast.

Tips & Tricks

  • Do not overmix the batter, a few lumps are ok.
  • Cook over medium heat to prevent burning.
  • Put cinnamon in batter, rather than on cooked pancake.
  • Put butter on after cooked to add more flavor.

The Recipe

Total time: 25 minutes

Servings: 4 people

Type Ingredient or Step Amount or Instructions
Ingredient Pancake Mix The entire box (Usually 32oz)
Ingredient Milk 1 cup
Ingredient Cinnamon Enough to cover entire batter
Ingredient Melted Butter 1/5 of a 4oz Stick
Ingredient Water 3/4 Cup
Step 1 Mix pancake mix with wet ingredients Combine pancake mix with milk and water in a large bowl to make batter have a slime-like texture.
Step 2 Add flavor ingredients First, mix melted butter until stirred fully, then add cinnamon until fully covering the batter.
Step 3 Heat the pan Lightly grease a skillet and heat over medium heat.
Step 4 Cook pancakes Pour in the batter. Flip when bubbles evenly appear throughout middle of pancake and until both sides are golden.
Step 5 Serve Serve warm with syrup, whipped cream, or whatever topping you like.

Homemade Brownies

The Story Behind the Recipe

I chose homemade brownies because it's affordable and a perfect dessert for an entire family. Chocolate brownies and vanilla ice creame make a sweet dessert that can be eaten throughout an entire week.

In my example, brownies are a familiar dessert served when there are holidays are sports events.

Tips & Tricks

  • Melt butter for fudginess
  • Whip the eggs and sugar
  • Do not overmix
  • Add a flavor enhancer
  • Cool completely before slicing

The Recipe

Total time:30 minutes

Servings:4 people

Type Ingredient or Step Amount or Instructions
Ingredient Brownie Mix Entire Box (18oz)
Ingredient Eggs 2-3
Ingredient Vanilla Extract 2 teaspoons
Ingredient Vegatable Oil 2 tablespoons
Ingredient Water 3 tablespoons
Step 1 Preheat and Prepare Preheat oven to 350 degrees F and lightly grease an 8x8 or 9x9 inch baking pan with PAM grease spray or whatever grease of choice.
Step 2 Combine Main Ingredients In a medium bowl, pour brownie mix, add water, and whisk together eggs to stir until fully combined.
Step 3 Add other Wet ingredients Pour vegetable oil and vanilla extract and mix until fully mixed in with brownie mix.
Step 4 Pour and Bake Spread batter evenly into baking pan and bake at 350 degrees F for 30 minutes.
Step 5 Cool and Slice After 30 minutes, put fork or knife into brownies, if chocolate is on the utensil, it may be undercooked. When satisfied with brownie, cut evenly into squares and let them cool.
